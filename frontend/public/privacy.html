Privacy Policy

Biz Tech Analytics — Resource Payout & Invoice Automation Platform. Last updated: 2 September 2026.

This internal platform is operated by Biz Tech Analytics ("we", "us") to onboard freelance resources, generate their invoices, and process their payouts. This policy explains what information the platform handles and why. It applies only to people who are given an account on the platform (administrators and invited resources).

Information we collect

  • Account & contact details: name, email address, postal address, phone number.
  • Tax & payment details: PAN, bank account number, IFSC code, beneficiary name, bank name.
  • Identity & agreement documents that a resource uploads: Aadhaar, PAN card, photograph, bank proof (passbook / cancelled cheque), signed NDA, and signed ICA.
  • Work & payout records: project, batch, hours, rate, invoice amounts, approval and payment status.

How we use it

  • To verify a resource's identity and eligibility to be paid.
  • To generate invoices and payout documents.
  • To execute bank transfers and reconcile them against bank statements.
  • To send transactional email notifications (invitations, document verification results, payout confirmations) related to these activities.

We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it for any purpose unrelated to paying resources.

Google Workspace / Drive access

The platform uses Google APIs to read a payout tracking spreadsheet and to create, populate, and store invoice and identity documents in Google Drive folders that we control. It accesses only the specific spreadsheet and Drive folders configured for this purpose. Google user data obtained through these APIs is used solely to provide the invoicing and payout features described above and is not transferred to others except as required to provide the service or by law.

Third-party processors

  • Google — Sheets, Drive, and Docs, for reading payout data and storing generated documents.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional email.
  • Neon — managed PostgreSQL database hosting.
  • Upstash — managed Redis, for the job queue and login sessions.
  • Render and Vercel — application hosting.

Retention

Records are retained for as long as needed to administer payouts and to meet tax, accounting, and legal obligations. Identity documents may be removed on request once they are no longer required for verification or compliance.
